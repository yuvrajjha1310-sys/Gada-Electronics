About Gada Electronics

Our Story

Engineering Reliability Since 1998

For over 28 years, Gada Electronics has been a trusted name in electrical and electronic equipment manufacturing and distribution. What began as a small trading operation has grown into a full-scale engineering and solutions provider serving power generation, manufacturing, infrastructure, and automation sectors across India.

Today, we combine deep technical expertise with a curated portfolio of the world's leading electrical brands to deliver equipment, installations, and support that industries can depend on — safely, efficiently, and on schedule.
