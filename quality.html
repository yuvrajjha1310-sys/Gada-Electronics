Quality Assurance

Our Commitment

Built On Four Quality Pillars

Every product and installation we deliver passes through a disciplined quality framework, from incoming inspection to final commissioning.

01

Sourcing Integrity

Only authorized-dealer sourcing from verified manufacturers — zero grey-market stock.

02

Incoming Inspection

03

Our Process

Quality Control Workflow

1. Vendor & Material Qualification

Every supplier is audited and every material batch is traced back to certified mill/factory test certificates before approval.

2. Incoming Quality Inspection

Dimensional checks, visual inspection, and sample electrical testing are performed on every incoming consignment.

3. Laboratory Testing

Insulation resistance, dielectric strength, and thermal cycling tests are run on critical switchgear and panels.

4. Controlled Storage & Handling

Climate-controlled warehousing and traceable batch handling prevent degradation before dispatch.

5. Site Testing & Commissioning

Final functional testing and safety sign-off happen on-site before any installation is handed over to the client.
